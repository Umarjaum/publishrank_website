01

A connected publication

PublishRank’s schema suite includes article and publisher identity tools. Start with the publication name, logo and author information so the same entities connect across your content.

02

Match markup to the content

Choose article types that match the page. Review NewsArticle, ReportageNewsArticle and BlogPosting settings against your actual editorial output. Validate the rendered JSON-LD after configuration.

03

Make validation routine

Use representative URLs to check your output after a theme change or plugin update. Remove duplicate schema owners where necessary and keep visible dates and authors aligned with the markup.
