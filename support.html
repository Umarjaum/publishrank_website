A useful support report includes

  1. The affected public URL or documentation page.
  2. Your WordPress and PublishRank versions, if relevant.
  3. What you expected and what actually happened.
  4. Minimal steps to reproduce the issue.
  5. Redacted screenshots or logs without credentials or personal information.

Security and privacy reports

Email the developer directly with a high-level description. Do not publish exploit details or send sensitive records. Ask for an appropriate channel before sharing material that could expose a system or a person.

For accessibility feedback, include the affected page, device or assistive technology, and the task you could not complete.
