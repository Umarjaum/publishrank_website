One mission, every size of newsroom.

Our goal is to handle the complete publisher SEO workflow: technical foundations, article recommendations, news discovery, content relationships, and ongoing operations. We focus on useful tools and clear documentation, not a profit motive.

We describe capabilities honestly, distinguish planned work from shipped work, and encourage testing against your actual publishing environment. “Nonprofit” describes the project's mission; it does not claim registered charity or tax-exempt status.
